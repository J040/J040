## Featured research 🔭

### [Look up to the sky and see](https://github.com/J040/look-up-to-the-sky-and-see)

A Python research project exploring image processing and the morphological classification of stars and galaxies in astronomical images.

My undergraduate research involved implementing an algorithm to segment galaxies and stars to support the study of galaxy clusters. I received a one-year scholarship at UNIVALI's Laboratory of Applied Intelligence and published and presented **“A study on the identification of galaxy clusters”** at Computer on the Beach in 2020.

**Research tools:** Python, pandas, NumPy, OpenCV, scikit-image and scikit-learn.
